📊 About Gold Rates

Gold prices are quoted in various units and currencies worldwide. The most common units are:

Troy Ounce (oz): The standard unit for precious metals trading internationally.

Gram: Commonly used in jewelry markets, especially in Asia and the Middle East.

Tola: Traditional unit used in South Asian countries like India and Pakistan.

Prices fluctuate based on market conditions, economic factors, and global demand and supply.
