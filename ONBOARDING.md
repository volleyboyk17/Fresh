## 🤔 Common Questions

### Q: Who should I ask if I'm stuck?
**A:** Your assigned mentor is your first point of contact. Don't hesitate to ask questions - we'd rather help you early than have you struggle alone!

### Q: How do I know what to work on?
**A:** Check the ClickUp board for tasks assigned to you, or ask your team lead for guidance on priorities.

### Q: What if I break something?
**A:** Don't worry! We have safeguards in place. All changes go through code review, and we have comprehensive testing. Mistakes are learning opportunities.

### Q: How often should I commit my code?
**A:** Commit early and often! Small, focused commits are better than large ones. Follow our commit message conventions.
